## Do the promises match the app?

- **Paywall benefits** (`app_en.arb:354-356`): "all four AI coaches, in English and Spanish" (true); "Voice practice: speak and hear replies on your device" (true on iOS where on-device recognition exists; voice is hidden elsewhere); "Your weekly plan and progress, kept on your device" (true, though the plan is a simple 3-checkbox list). The fair-use line matches the backend constants.
- **App Store description** (`docs/appstore/metadata.md` §1): the four coaches, bio help, openers and replies, stalled conversations, first-date planning, "Follow a short weekly plan and see your progress", hold-to-talk voice, privacy bullets, fair use and the $19.99 price all exist in code. Two lines depend on undeployed chat-v7: "You will see a notice in the chat when you are close to a limit" and the App Review note "Sandbox purchases have a smaller limit of 20 replies a day". Nothing promises photo review, video avatars or role-play scoring. Screenshot captions say "Example chat". The review notes say messages go to "Meta Llama 3.3 70B", which matches the Worker's `/health` note; the deployed model still needs checking (known retired-model issue).

## Top 5 gaps that would surprise a tester

1. **Every chat fails today on a real build.** The live Worker is chat-v5 with a retired model (502), so the core feature shows "Coaching is temporarily unavailable" until chat-v7 is deployed. The limit notices, the JWS check and the sandbox cap described to App Review also exist only in chat-v7.
2. **A fake-store review build cannot talk to the real coach.** It sends no entitlement token, so chat-v7 returns 403 SUBSCRIPTION_REQUIRED ("subscription inactive" notice) unless it is built with `DW_MOCK_AI=true`. Its replies are then scripted demo text, not AI.
3. **Weekly plan items do nothing when tapped except tick.** Their chat prompts (`planPrompt*`) are written in both languages but never wired up. Ticks are manual and unrelated to what the user actually did in chat, even though Progress says "Counted on this device from what you actually did".
4. **"Practice a conversation" is not a practice mode.** It is a single canned chat message. Role-play, feedback and reply chips all depend on Llama following prompt rules, so results vary between replies.
5. **Mixed-language surfaces and voice limits.** With an English UI and Sofía or Diego, the starters, greeting and thread titles are Spanish while the Home tiles send English prompts. Voice input only works where iOS has on-device recognition for that language (otherwise "language unsupported"), and spoken quality depends on the voices installed on the phone. Also check the hosted privacy and support pages: the in-app privacy text was updated on Nov 24 after the Lovable pages were checked against the Nov 23 text.
