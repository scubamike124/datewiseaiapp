## Part 1: Crisis numbers

### What the prompt says today

`backend/coach_prompts.js:140` and `lib/services/persona.dart:59` (the two copies match):

> "...give a crisis line (US: call or text 988; Mexico: Línea de la Vida 800 911 2000; danger: 911)... point to help (US hotline 1-800-799-7233)."

Correction to the brief: the prompt does **not** contain "text START to 88788". It gives only the phone number 1-800-799-7233.

### Recommended wording, one block per country (for the system prompt)

- **US:** "Call or text 988 (Suicide & Crisis Lifeline, free, 24/7, Spanish available). If you are in immediate danger, call 911. For abuse or a controlling partner: National Domestic Violence Hotline, 1-800-799-7233, or text START to 88788."
- **Canada:** "Call or text 9-8-8 (Suicide Crisis Helpline, 24/7, English or French). If you are in immediate danger, call 9-1-1. For family or gender-based violence, contact your province's or territory's crisis line, or 9-1-1 in an emergency."
- **México:** "Llama a la Línea de la Vida, 800 911 2000 (gratuita, las 24 horas, los 365 días). Si estás en peligro inmediato, llama al 911. Si vives violencia de género, llama a la Línea de las Mujeres: marca 079 y elige la opción 1 (gratuita y confidencial, las 24 horas)."
- **España:** "Llama al 024, la línea de atención a la conducta suicida (gratuita, confidencial, las 24 horas). Si hay un peligro inmediato, llama al 112. Si sufres violencia machista, llama al 016 (gratuito, las 24 horas, no aparece en la factura; WhatsApp 600 000 016). Recuerda borrar la llamada del historial del móvil si lo necesitas."
- **Unknown country:** list the four suicide lines and emergency numbers in one line: "US 988 / Canada 9-8-8 / México 800 911 2000 / España 024; emergencies 911 (US, Canada, Mexico) or 112 (Spain)."

Implementation notes:
- Keep the server copy (`coach_prompts.js`) and the client copy (`persona.dart`) identical. A test that compares them is cheap insurance.
- Add a **static "Get help now" screen** in the You tab that shows these numbers without the AI. A model can hallucinate digits or be unreachable (F3), and a static screen is the reliable fallback. Add tests that check the digits.
