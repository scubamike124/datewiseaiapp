## Per-image notes

- Eyes: olive/greenish-hazel at 2x, not dark brown. Catchlights are present but faint and slightly different shapes. The lower lashes look clumped and drawn on.
- Teeth: visible gap and uneven incisor widths, dark voids at both mouth corners. The upper row looks narrow for the smile width. Passable at phone size, odd at full size.
- Skin: dense, uniform red-dot freckle "spray" across the whole face, neck and chest. It reads as a noise pattern, not natural freckle clustering. Nose bridge is smoothed.
- Hair: looks wet and greasy, with stringy clumps and a flat, damp crown. Untidy for a coach.
- Clothes: the left collar fold is soft and mushy, and the placket buttons are fine. The background window frame is fine.
- Looks younger than 28 and slightly dishevelled. Not professional enough.

## Identity consistency
Within sets, a1 and a2 match each other, and b1 and b2 are close. Across sets, the a, b and c faces are noticeably different people: the c1 face is round, the b2 face is narrow, and the a set has a longer jaw. Pick ONE seed or identity for both hero and avatar. Do not mix sets.

2. **b1**: warm, good teeth and brown eyes, friendly. Pairs with b2 if a smiling variant is needed.
