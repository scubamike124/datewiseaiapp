## 3. What the matrix says (one paragraph each)

- **Only three hosted options are plausible inside the owner's $4-5 budget at 60 minutes:** Simli ($1.85), bitHuman on-device ($0.95, native SDK) and bitHuman cloud ($4.32, borderline). Anam is the quality leader on paper but costs $10.83 at list; it needs the enterprise rate (about $0.04, unquoted) or a small capped allowance.
- **No hosted vendor has published lip-sync accuracy or Spanish lip-sync quality.** Every row says UNVERIFIED because it is: the only way to know is the pilot with our portraits and es-MX lines.
- **Privacy diverges sharply.** Anam publishes SOC 2 Type II, DPA and a no-training statement; Simli publishes only an Enterprise ZDR/SLA line; the rest are unread. Reading the actual terms of the chosen vendor is a gate before any real user traffic.
- **Open options are cheap per minute but carry unresolved licences (FlashHead, MuseTalk training data) and a warm-GPU floor.** They stay a scale hedge, not a replacement for a proven API.
