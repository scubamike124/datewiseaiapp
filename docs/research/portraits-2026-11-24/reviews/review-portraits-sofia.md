# Sofía portrait review (independent photo-editor pass)

Date: 2026-11-24. Six candidates, 896x1152, reviewed at full size plus 2x crops of the eyes, mouth and earrings
(crops in `scratchpad/crops-sofia-review/`).

Brief: about 29, Mexican woman, long dark brown wavy hair, DARK BROWN eyes (strict), small gold hoop earrings,
warm smile, professional and approachable, must read as a real photograph.

**Overall verdict: none of the six is clean enough to ship as-is.** Every image has at least one AI tell at
100% (earrings, hair halo or pendant). b2 and a2 are closest. No candidate has *small* hoops: all hoops are
medium to large, and a1 has no hoops at all.

## Per-image review

- Photorealism 6/10. Skin 6/10: some pores and freckles, but cheeks and forehead are smoothed.
- Eyes: medium-to-dark warm brown (amber tint in the light). **Left eye (viewer's left) has a malformed pupil and
  catchlight**: a bright, off-centre blob rather than a clean reflection. Catchlights don't match between the eyes.
  The eyes are slightly narrowed, which is natural for a smile.
- Teeth 7/10: even, plausible, slightly too uniform.
- Hair 4/10: long, dark brown and wavy as briefed, but **a heavy backlit frizz halo of detached "spaghetti" strands**
  floats around the head and shoulders (top, right side, right shoulder). A classic AI tell.
- Earrings 3/10: **off brief (large hammered teardrops, not small hoops) and mismatched.** The left one is a smooth
  embossed leaf and the right one is crumpled, with a different texture and shape.
- Clothing/background: blouse and buttons fine; the bokeh and plants are fine. Strong orange grade.
- Professionalism 7/10. Crop: face centred, good for a banner; the circle avatar works but the hair halo is busy.
- Respect: fine. The terracotta wall, plants, big gold earrings and orange grade together lean toward a
  "warm Mexican postcard" palette. It's not offensive, but it's a cliché worth avoiding in the final set.

## Ranking: best 3 that can plausibly be the same person (group A)

1. **b2**: best match to the brief (long dark wavy hair, dark brown eyes, centred). Fixes needed: retouch or remove
   the malformed pendant and match the hoop sizes (ideally make them smaller).
2. **a2**: most natural and approachable daytime image. Caveats: shoulder-length hair, medium (not dark) brown
   irises, mismatched hoops, blob pendant. Only usable if the owner accepts medium brown, or after an iris darken.
3. **a1**: same face, but only after fixes: it needs the earrings replaced with small hoops, the hair halo cleaned
   up and the left pupil/catchlight repaired. As-is, it is a hold.

## Must not be shown

- **c2**: malformed earrings, merging necklaces, frizz halo and odd catchlights. Obviously AI.
- **b1**: different person; sheer lace top is the wrong register for a coaching app; lopsided smirk.
- **c1**: malformed earrings and a different person (group C). Don't mix it into Sofía's set. It has the best skin
  realism, so it's a useful reference for the regeneration prompt.
- **a1 as-is**: off-brief earrings, mismatched pair, hair halo, malformed pupil.

## Recommendation

Regenerate (or inpaint) from the b2/a2 face with: *small* matching gold huggie/hoop earrings, no pendant (or a
plain fine chain), long dark wavy hair with backlight reduced to avoid frizz halos, dark brown irises, a neutral
daytime setting (not terracotta-and-cactus, not nightlife), and a solid-colour blouse. Then re-check the earrings
and catchlights at 200%.
