# Ethan portrait review (independent, strict)

Reviewed 2026-11-24. Six candidates, 896x1152. Each image was viewed at full frame, then the eye and mouth regions were cropped and enlarged 3x (and b2's hands 2x).
Target: about 32, short tousled dark brown hair, DARK BROWN eyes (strict), light stubble, friendly relaxed smile, professional.

## Overall verdict

None of the six would pass for an untouched photograph under close inspection. They all have the same SDXL look: over-sharpened HDR micro-contrast,
deep crow's-feet and under-eye creases etched too hard (all of them look 36-42, not 32), and noisy fabric texture.
At small-circle size a1, a2 and c1 read as believable photos. At hero-banner size every one shows a tell.

**Eye colour (strict gate):** only **c1** has irises that read as clearly dark brown. a1, a2 and c2 are medium or warm brown. b1 is light amber or hazel-brown and **fails**.
b2 is dark reddish-brown but the eyes are squinted and small. If the gate is strictly "dark brown", **c1 is the only pass**. c2 is the next closest, then a2.

### a1: indoor, grey speckled crewneck, white tee, window frame
- Eyes: **medium warm brown, slightly amber**, not dark brown. Reasonably symmetric. A catchlight in both eyes. Glassy wet artefact on the lower lid of the image-right eye.
- Skin: real pores and freckles, but the crow's feet and under-eye bags are over-etched and age him to about 38.
- Teeth: good. Slightly narrow but even and natural-looking. The best set of teeth here.
- Hair: matches the brief well (short, tousled, dark brown). A few wispy flyaways at the crown look painted.
- Clothing: the sweater knit is AI "confetti" noise with random gold and blue flecks and no coherent knit structure. Obvious when viewed large.
- Background: plain wall and window frame, clean. The dark object at the far left edge (about x 0-10, y 590-620) is unexplained but minor.
- Crop: face is centred with headroom. Very good for both the hero banner and the circle avatar.

- Skin: same over-etched wrinkles. Forehead texture is slightly crunchy.

- Background: plausible. The window mullion is clean.

## Recommendation
Nothing here passes the owner's "real photograph" bar unretouched. The strongest path is to regenerate in the a1 setting with c1's eye colour, prompting for softer under-eye detail, age 32 and a fine-knit sweater.
If something must ship now, use c1 for the small circle avatar and a1 (with the irises darkened) for the hero. a1 and c1 are not perfectly identical, so the owner should confirm the identity match.
