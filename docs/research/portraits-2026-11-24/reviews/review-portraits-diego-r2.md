# Diego portrait review, round 2 (independent photo-editor pass)

Brief: Mexican man about 31-33, short black hair, short neat full beard, DARK BROWN eyes (strict), calm kind smile, professional and approachable. Must read as a real photograph.

Method: viewed each full 896x1152 frame, then 2-3x enlarged crops of eyes, mouths, the v3c1 chest logo and the v3c2 ear (crops in `scratchpad/crops/diego-*-eyes.png`, `diego-r2-mouths.png`, `v3c1-logo.png`, `v3c2-ear.png`). Iris colour judged visually on the enlarged crops; pixel samples back it up (iris/pupil means all fall in very dark warm-brown, V 0.03-0.21).

Overall verdict: this round is an improvement in beard density (all four new images have full beards, no patchiness), but **only v3c1 actually looks early 30s**, and **over-sharpening is still present in v3a1 and v3a2**. Two of the four new images have defects that block use as-is.

No stereotyping or disrespectful content in any of the six: plain linen shirts and polos, park/street backgrounds, no costume props, no caricature. Fine.
