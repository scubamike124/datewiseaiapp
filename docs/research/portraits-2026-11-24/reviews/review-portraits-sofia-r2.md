# Sofía portraits, round 2: independent photo-editor review

Reviewer: independent subagent, 2026-11-24. I viewed each image at full size and checked the eyes, mouth, jewellery and clothing in 3x zoomed crops. Iris colours come from looking at the image and from sampled pixel means. The sampled values are low-light averages and include some pupil and lash shadow, so the visual call below is the one to rely on.

Target: about 29, Mexican woman, long dark brown wavy hair, DARK BROWN eyes (strict), warm smile, professional, no jewellery, neutral cafe or restaurant background.

**Headline:** round 3 is a real improvement in photorealism and background, but it is not clean. **3 of the 4 new images still show jewellery** (v3a1 a tiny stud, v3b1 a broken necklace, v3b2 an earring and a chain). The "b" pair moved to a dark bar with bokeh light orbs, which looks like a date night, not a neutral cafe. **No new image has truly dark brown eyes**; all are medium brown to amber. Only the old b2 is properly dark.

---

## sofia-v3a1.png  (8/10, BEST)
- **Photorealism:** 8. Reads like a real window-light portrait, with natural shallow depth of field.
- **Skin:** 8. Real texture, light freckles, pores, a slight natural sheen. Not airbrushed, mildly sharpened but acceptable.
- **Apparent age:** 26-29. On target.
- **Eyes:** warm **medium brown** (chestnut, not dark brown). The pair is symmetric, with one natural window catchlight in each eye in the same position. Artefacts: one stray vertical lash or dark hairline crosses the top of her right iris (image left); there is a tiny orange speck at the outer corner of each eye (it reads as the tear duct/caruncle and is acceptable). **Darkening needed: mild (about 15-20%).**
- **Teeth/mouth:** natural, even and white without being veneer-perfect. The lips are slightly glossy. No defects.
- **Hair:** long, dark brown, wavy, with lighter caramel strands. The left-side strands are a little stringy and wet-looking, and a few flyaways are over-sharpened at the top right. Acceptable.
- **Jewellery:** **one small stud earring on her left ear (image right)**. It is a blurry two-tone blob, not cleanly formed. It breaks the no-jewellery brief and needs a small clone-out retouch.
- **Hands:** none visible.
- **Clothing/background:** white button collar blouse. The collar is asymmetric (one side folded flatter), which is plausible. The background is a blurred window and street with wooden frames: neutral, not clichéd. No text or logo artefacts.
- **Professionalism:** high. Approachable and natural.
- **Crop:** face centred with headroom. Works as a hero banner and as a circle avatar (the face fills the circle well).

## sofia-v3a2.png  (6.5/10)

- **Skin:** 7.5. Freckles and texture, slight over-sharpening on the forehead.

A peasant-style blouse on a Mexican character leans mildly towards the folkloric cliché, and it reads casual/boho rather than professional.
- **Background:** blurred window, fine.
- **Professionalism:** medium (casual top, teeth).
- **Crop:** face slightly left of centre and smaller in frame. OK for a circle, weaker for a hero banner.

## Ranking (best three overall, as alternatives)
1. **sofia-v3a1.png.** The most real-looking, professional and on-brief image. It needs only (a) the tiny stud earring cloned out and (b) a mild darkening of the irises to dark brown.
2. **sofia-v3b2.png.** Strong face at the right age. It needs the earring and chain removed, the irises darkened and matched, and ideally the scalp crop extended.
3. **sofia-v3a2.png.** No jewellery and a good setting, but it **needs the teeth whitened and cleaned up**, and the irises darkened. It looks younger than 29 and the blouse lace is melted. (sofia-b2.png is the alternative third choice if the eye colour matters more than the retouch effort, but its hoops and pendant are heavy to remove.)

- **sofia-b2.png**: not as is (malformed pendant, hoops). Only after the jewellery is removed.

## Eye colour
**Yes, darkening is needed on every new image.** v3a1 needs mild darkening, v3a2 moderate, v3b2 moderate plus matching the two irises, and v3b1 strong. The target is about the depth of b2's irises (very dark brown, nearly black at the limbus), keeping the existing catchlights and texture.

## Stereotype and respect check
Round 3 removed the terracotta/adobe and hoop cliché, which is a real improvement. v3a2's embroidered peasant blouse leans mildly folkloric. None of the four new images is disrespectful.
