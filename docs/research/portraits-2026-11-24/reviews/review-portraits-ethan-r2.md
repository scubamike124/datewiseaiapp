# Ethan portraits, round 2 (v3) review

Reviewer: independent photo-editor pass, 2026-11-24. Full frames viewed plus 2x crops of the eye and mouth area
(`scratchpad/crops-r2/`). Iris colours are judged by eye from the crops. Automated pixel sampling mostly hit pupils
and is not reliable here.

Target: about 30-32, short tousled dark brown hair, dark brown eyes (strict), light stubble, relaxed smile,
professional and approachable. Previous rejection reasons: over-sharpened HDR look, deep etched wrinkles (reads 36-42),
bad teeth.

**Overall verdict: this round is only partly better.** Teeth improved in v3a1 and v3a2. The etched crow's feet and
over-sharpened hair and knit texture are still there in every image. None of the four is a clean pass on all three
earlier rejection points.

## Ranking: best 3, all the same person (Group A)

1. **ethan-v3a2.png.** Best overall in this identity, with warm, natural light and acceptable teeth. It needs the iris
   darkened (light amber/hazel to dark brown), the crow's feet and forehead lines softened about 40-50%, and the
   sweater flecks desaturated.
2. **ethan-a1.png.** The same face with slightly better eyes, but the same deep crow's feet. It needs the same
   wrinkle softening and minor iris darkening.
3. **ethan-v3c2.png.** Use only as an outdoor or lifestyle alternate, after edits: soften the skin and wrinkles,
   darken the iris and check the teeth. Otherwise it is too old and too squinty. Treat it as a weak third.

If the owner prefers a younger-looking Ethan, **v3a1** is the most age-appropriate and has the best teeth. It is a
different person, though, so it cannot be mixed with Group A. Choosing it means regenerating a set around that face,
and its cheek speck and hair would need fixing first.

## Advice for the next generation

Lower the sharpening and detail strength. Add "smooth natural skin, minimal smile lines" or negative-prompt "wrinkles,
crow's feet, HDR". Use a plain or merino knit to avoid fleck artefacts, and write "deep dark brown eyes" rather than
"brown". Prefer a soft indoor window light. Outdoor sun produced the worst results.
